GOAL: Add a Tier + Evolution system to weapons. A weapon at MAX level (level 5 after spec #1; if spec #1 is not yet merged, treat max as the weapon's `maxLevel`) can EVOLVE to a next-tier weapon when the player opens a Treasure Chest. Evolving swaps the equipped weapon's id/behavior to the evolved form and RESETS its level to 1. The chest draft offers EVOLVE as the FIRST card when any equipped weapon is evolvable. All selection of which weapon/evolution to offer uses the seeded rng so it stays deterministic.
