# Measured basis and limits

Local investigation, 2026-09-10 UTC. This summary contains no private source
assets or machine-specific paths; the skill and its tests work independently.

Historical raw potion and eyewear files were fully opaque despite explicit
transparency prompts. Checkerboard could remain inside glass after corrective
edits cleared broader areas. Other outputs had genuine alpha despite misleading
raw previews. These findings rule out treating the extension, preview or prompt
as proof of transparency; they do not identify a hidden backend cause.

A fresh empty-bottle trial had real alpha and visibly transmitting glass. A
follow-up batch generated one fresh example for each of eight other subjects:

| Subject | Observed behavior in that single trial |
| --- | --- |
| Filled potion | Upper glass transmitted; sampled liquid about 99% opaque. |
| Fishing net | Open cells and thin cords worked visually; sampled hole had alpha-1 residue. |
| Hair | Gaps and wisps preserved; tiny residual values in sampled opening. |
| Candy bottle | Clear upper glass and opaque white candy worked; two apparent inter-candy gaps contained near-opaque fill. |
| Eyeglasses | Sampled lenses passed roughly 80–82% source-over background contribution. |
| Glowing potion | Glow faded correctly; liquid and sampled upper glass nearly opaque. |
| Sheer cloth | Sampled panels passed only about 1.5–14.5% background contribution. |
| Stained-glass lantern | Clear handle opening; sampled colored panes about 99% opaque. |

All eight follow-up files had genuine alpha; no baked checkerboard or solid
rectangle appeared in the reviewed composites. Six had alpha-1 border residue.
There was no prompt A/B test or repeated reliability benchmark. These are
observations of particular files, not universal model behavior or recommended
opacity settings. Background contribution means `1 - alpha/255`, not measured
physical optical transmittance. The lasting lesson is to verify exterior alpha,
holes and material behavior separately.
